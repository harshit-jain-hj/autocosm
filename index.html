Autocosm /01 — Capital
Verissimus / Long-only India

The artistry
in capital.

The first
conviction.

I was the kid who told everyone at 15 that I would have the biggest hedge fund in the world.

But the only reason I fell in love with finance was because I saw the artistry within it.

I began in 2014–15. At NYU, I invested through Stern IAG. Today I manage Verissimus in a couple of hours a day at most, alongside building Dyeus.

Every company is a story with an open ending.

And I’m not a finance bro. I’m a worldbuilder.
